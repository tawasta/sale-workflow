=======================================
Sales Team: Show Team Members as a List
=======================================

* Replaces kanban grid of Sales Team's members with a simple list
* As a bonus byproduct, this resolves issue where a user with 
  Sales Administrator access rights could not see the team
  member removal button in the kanban view unless they 
  had Settings access rights as well.

Configuration
=============
* None needed

Usage
=====
* Just open a Sales Team form view to see the member list.
